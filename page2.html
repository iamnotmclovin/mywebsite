A Guide to Hingus

The first step to Hingushood is denying the self. You must realize that you are flawed inherently, but Hingushood is perfect.

Next, you must turn towards servitude. Hingus spent his life making things better for others at any expense. Hingushood is helping.

Lastly, you must embrace the community. Hingus understood that individual growth is meaningless without contributing to the greater good. Hingushood is unity.
